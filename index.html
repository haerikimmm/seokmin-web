Seoul, Korea Trained as a metalsmith, then spent two years running a public fab lab. Now works between CNC, injection moulds and a TIG torch. The same problem in three materials.

Selected work

01 / Plaster & microcement

Seoul

Microcement goes on in thin coats and gives you one window to work it. The counter is the difficult piece: a folded plane with an inside corner and a long unsupported edge, floated and burnished by hand so the arris stays sharp and the field reads flat under raking light. No two square metres cure the same.

03 / Precious Plastic

Seoul · Vientiane

The mould is the real object. Every part starts as an aluminium block on the CNC, and what comes out of it is shredded household plastic, melted, pressed, and cooled into something with a second use. In Vientiane the machines themselves were the deliverable: built on site from parts available there, handed over, still running. Recycling only scales if the equipment does.
